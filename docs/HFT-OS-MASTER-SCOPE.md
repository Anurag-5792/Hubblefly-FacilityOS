## 1. Product purpose and boundaries
HFT-OS must operate the company through connected people, material, documents, business events, policy, automation and management intelligence. It is not a CRUD demonstration, a collection of spreadsheets, or a chatbot with an inventory UI. Users should perform ordinary Stores work quickly with QR scans, bulk selections and clear exceptions, with management able to see reliable operational facts and take informed decisions.

- **Now:** deliver working Hubblefly Stores with actual inventory visibility, master data, locations, serial/batch custody, inward/outward/return/transfer/count and the supporting documents, quality, access and reports. Minimum Employee information necessary for Stores belongs here.
- **Next:** extend the functioning operational foundation into deeper procurement, costing, vendor intelligence, manufacturing genealogy, dispatch and management automation. Basic intelligence is designed into R1; advanced functions are incremental, not a precondition for stock visibility or first qualified Stores transactions.
- **Later:** integrated HRMS, attendance, leave, payroll, broader company operations and ERPNext/finance integration. Full HRMS is explicitly deferred from the immediate Stores release. Confirm detailed module policies before implementing new later-phase functions.
- HFT is a distinct legal entity. In Phase 1, one authoritative HFT PostgreSQL stock ledger owns quantity, location, custody and movements. ERPNext stock/accounting integration and financial reconciliation are Phase 2. Do not create a second operational stock writer or connect HFT runtime to another company's HR/inventory database.
- Reuse-first: inspect existing AISupport corporate assets, schemas, workflow patterns and compatible components before rebuilding; review rights, data ownership, authorization and quality; adapt approved code locally. No blind copying, external operational data sync, or import of another company's records. Keep confidential reuse dossiers in restricted storage, not this public repository.

### Physical location and inventory semantics
Legal entity → Site → Warehouse → Rack/approved Area → Level/Shelf → Position → Stack Slot → Bin/Container → Item + Product Version + Batch/Serial. Use actual active leaf locations; preserve BN bins, BX cartons, BB battery cartons and approved physically applicable exceptions. A movable container identity is not a permanent location. Preserve original placement and movement history; never invent missing physical identities or revive retired identifiers.

Keep physical arrival, QC acceptance, recorded observations, approved availability, custody, consumption, damaged/quarantine and count reconciliation distinct. Quantities across unlike UOMs must not be added as one stock KPI. Raw imported reports and provisional QR/sticker records are not proof of issued serials, certified placements or current balances.

## 3. QR-first, not QR-only (R1-14)
Normal Stores operations should be executable with a handheld keyboard-wedge QR scanner and, where supported, camera scanning. The interface maintains the expected scan context, advances automatically, accepts rapid multi-item scans, gives immediate visual/audible acknowledgement, and supports clear correction/cancel/confirmation commands. No keyboard/mouse should be necessary for the normal scan path; keyboard and accessible manual entry remain available for exceptions and bulk selections. Do not treat an employee QR as authentication or a human approval.

- **Issue example:** authenticated operator selects/scans transaction type → employee/work-order QR → source location/container → item/version → batch/quantity or serial selection → additional lines → document/purpose reference → reviewed confirmation.
- **Receipt example:** supplier and purchase/invoice/challan references → item/version → actual batch/serial or pack/quantity → destination leaf → verified QC/approval → reviewed confirmation.
- **Transfer/return/count:** scan applicable original document, source/destination, employee, item and serial/batch, condition/evidence references according to the governed transaction. Count stays nonposting.
- **Serial range:** BOTH start and end fields accept scan or keyboard entry interchangeably. Scan first → automatic focus to last → scan last → build inclusive selection preview. Recognize scanner terminator without a separate mouse/key action. Multiple ranges plus individual scans may be combined. Preserve leading zeros, case/registered identity and exact numbering series. Only expand a recognized numeric series; arbitrary manufacturer serials require explicit selection. Resolve every identity against the registry. Detect overlaps, gaps, duplicate/unknown/retired/unissued/already-issued serials, wrong item/version, scope, location, custody, condition and operation eligibility. Report precise exceptions; no silent skipping or serial generation. Derive quantity from verified unique selections. Permit deliberate correction/exclusion with explicit review. Revalidate at commit under current locks, authority and document revision. Multi-serial posting requires qualified atomic semantics, not a client loop of independent single-serial writes.
- **Batch/pack:** scan verified batch or pack/container to resolve contents; allow typed/scanned quantities with approved UOM conversion and validation. No assumed pack quantity.
- **Documents:** decode supported QR/barcode formats, verify source/signature/lookup where applicable, extract supported fields, cross-check supplier, date, line identity, amounts/quantity and duplicate status. If missing or unsupported, offer controlled manual/document-capture fallback. Never claim all invoice, challan or e-way bill QRs carry complete data. Raw QR text is untrusted input, not executable instruction.
- Qualify using actual target scanner hardware, terminator settings, rapid scanning, invalid sequence, multiple ranges, session expiry, disconnection, duplicate/retry and concurrent inventory changes. Confirmation must be deliberate and recoverable; scanner actions do not bypass approvals.

## 5. Embedded operational intelligence and visual management (not a chatbot)
Owner intent: AI must help manage and understand the company’s material and document flow and management decisions continuously. A conversational assistant is not the required deliverable. Intelligence belongs within normal workflows, dashboards, alerts and decision records.

The common pattern is verified operational events → reconciled facts → deterministic policy/calculations → statistical/AI analysis → prioritized action, draft document or review task → authorized human decision where required → recorded outcome and follow-up. Use scheduled/event-triggered processing with idempotency, monitoring, retries only where safe and an exception queue. All recommendations show source references, as-of date, assumptions, confidence/limitations, reason and expected effect. Record actual decision and outcome to evaluate recommendation quality.

| INT-01 | Inventory flow | R1 basic visual balances/movements, pending/hold, custody, missing fields and discrepancy reports. Incrementally add consumption trends, abnormal usage, ageing, slow/nonmoving stock, projected stockouts and reorder suggestions from validated stock/lead-time/demand data. |
| INT-02 | Documentation | R1 source linking, validation and audit. Extend to document classification, structured extraction with source citations/field confidence, duplicate/mismatch checks, document drafting, naming/numbering and review routing, missing document reminders and evidence completeness. Show missing conversion/cost data rather than misleading comparisons. Accounting postings remain Phase 2. |
| INT-04 | Vendor selection | Evidence-backed scorecards comparing equivalent spec/quality, normalized cost, delivery/lead time, acceptance/reject rate, responsiveness, order fulfillment, commercial terms and capacity/risk where known. Do not hold the working Stores release for AI implementation. ## 7. Common operating controls and acceptance
Each workflow uses actual scoped masters and actor identity, explicit state transitions, effective-dated policy, source documents/evidence, validated calculations, authorized approval, persisted results, independent readback and audit. Preserve existing P0/P2/P3 protections, stock holds, maker/checker separation by actual person, single protected writer, immutable movements and original source facts. Do not alter applied migrations or directly update stock balances to make screens look complete. No blind retry after uncertain commit.

Mandatory R1 release evidence:
- Real master CREATE/GET/LIST/UPDATE and authorized activation/retirement, complete version/photo evidence, actual persisted database record/history, relogin and upgrade preserving data; scoped role assignments enforced.
- Actual verified opening subset with physical evidence, cut-off and nonduplicative import; master identity admission, photo/version activation, serial issuance and stock posting remain separate decisions.
- Real supplier invoice → GRN → independent QC/approval → receive five units into valid actual or clearly synthetic qualification bin; independent committed balance and location readback.
- Issue two units to valid employee/work order; available three, custody/consumption two, ACK_PENDING until genuine acknowledgement. Independently verify return, count with no posting, and transfer one unit between two bins, leaving available balances two and one if performed in that order.
- Actual scanner single/multiple/range selection including first and last QR, mixed ranges, gaps/duplicates, multi-item and atomic rollback; negative wrong scope, serial, location, UOM, hold, stale revision, QC, duplicate receipt, oversubscription and concurrent conflicts.
- Real TLS, restricted roles and DB authorization, source-bound verified CI/build, signed-session browser/mobile tests, idempotency and unknown-commit recovery, encrypted backup and independent clean restore, compatible rollback/upgrade with serial/custody and ledger reconciliation.
- Actual reports tie to accepted ledger facts and source records, consistent watermark and drill-down. Show evidence for both positive and negative controls.
- Source code, proposed migrations, tests on doubles, offline staging, UI mockups and technical health are not operational acceptance. Existing standing engineering authorization does not waive production/data/transaction-specific gates. No repeated routine owner approvals for already accepted scope.
