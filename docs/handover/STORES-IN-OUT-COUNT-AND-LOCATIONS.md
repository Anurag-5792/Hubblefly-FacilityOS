# Stores IN / OUT and physical location control

**DRAFT — not approved for live operations.**

This document explains the intended receiving, issuing, counting and stock-location process. Operators must follow only the version qualified and issued with the approved production release.

## Physical location

Each item is identified by its company, site, warehouse, rack or area, level/shelf, position, stack slot and bin or container where applicable. Confirm the actual storage address on the physical label or by scanning the QR/barcode. Items in different bins must retain distinct location records.

## Material IN

Identify the supplier and invoice or delivery document. Capture the actual item and version, unit, quantity, batch or serial, physical receiver, condition and exact destination storage location. Record any pending quality check or quarantine. Verify authorization and the confirmed receipt result before considering stock available for issue.

## Material OUT

Select an active employee or approved manufacturing work order and issue purpose. Locate the current available item at its exact source bin, confirm version/serial/batch and quantity, and verify applicable approvals. Submit the issue, confirm the resulting source balance and record custody or consumption. Employee acknowledgement remains pending until the actual recipient confirms possession.

## Physical counting

Start a controlled count for the correct warehouse and count period. Scan the location and item for each line, record the quantity and condition, and record identical items separately for each actual bin. Count results require discrepancy review and authorized reconciliation. A physical count itself does not create stock or transfer existing balances.

## Location changes

Material moved to another rack, level, position or bin requires a controlled transfer recording both source and destination. A displayed location must not simply be overwritten for stock already posted.

## Exceptions

Do not proceed with unknown item identity, a missing or invalid location, disputed serial, incorrect supplier evidence, pending quality disposition, blocked authorization, insufficient stock or an uncertain posting result. Report the exception and retain the original record for approved resolution.

**Availability:** These features remain unqualified until the real application, database, authorization and end-to-end operating tests are accepted.
